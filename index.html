B2B SAAS GROWTH & AI TRANSFORMATION

From 2M Vanity Visits to 40% Pipeline Contribution

Entity: Vantage Circle Products: 4 Multi-Product Suites Tenure: Sept 2020 – Present

warning The Algorithmic Disruption:

Prior to Google’s Helpful Content Updates (HCU) and AI Overviews, the website peaked at 2M monthly clicks driven by broad top-of-funnel HR guides. When Google began synthesizing zero-click AI answers, generic informational traffic plummeted across the SaaS ecosystem. Rather than panicking or chasing declining informational queries, I instituted a fundamental realignment: Informational traffic is vanity; qualified pipeline is sanity.

PHASE 01 content_cut

Aggressive Content Pruning

Audited hundreds of legacy articles via Airtable & Screaming Frog API. Retired and HTTP 410’d off-niche, non-converting blogs that diluted topical relevance, reallocating 100% of crawl equity back to core SaaS features.

Restored 100% Topical Cohesion
PHASE 02 hub

Topical Authority Mapping

Engineered comprehensive entity and topical authority maps across all 4 core SaaS product lines, mapping competitor content gaps directly to MoFU evaluation hubs and BoFU purchase decision pages.

Strict Semantic Internal Linking Silos
PHASE 03 ads_click

High-Intent Commercial Pivot

Rebuilt feature pages, comparison hubs, and competitor alternative matrices. Rather than answering basic 'what is' questions, content directly solved enterprise procurement criteria and pricing evaluations.

High-Intent BOFU Conversion Focus
PHASE 04 smart_toy

GEO & AI Citation Optimization

Structured semantic entity nodes, JSON-LD Schema (Organization, SoftwareApplication), and digital PR co-citations across G2 and Gartner so that AI engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) actively cite the brand.
