The Post-HCU & AEO Turnaround

How a strategic pivot from top-of-funnel informational traffic to high-converting commercial intent protected revenue and drove compounding YoY lead growth across 4 SaaS product lines.

B2B SaaS Growth & AI Transformation

From 2M Vanity Visits to 40% Pipeline Contribution

Company: Vantage Circle Products: Rewards & Recognition, Pulse, Perks, Fit Tenure: 2020 – Present

The Challenge: Prior to August 2023, the site enjoyed massive top-of-funnel reach peaking at 2M monthly clicks. However, Google’s Helpful Content Updates (HCU) and subsequent AI Overviews severely disrupted zero-click informational search. Broad blogs were getting summarized by Google AI without driving traffic. While many across the industry panicked, I recognized that informational traffic was vanity—qualified pipeline was sanity.

Phase 1: Aggressive Content Pruning

Audited hundreds of legacy articles. Retired and de-indexed off-niche, non-converting blogs that diluted topical relevance, reallocating crawl equity and consolidating authority back to core SaaS solutions.

Phase 2: Topical Authority Mapping

Engineered comprehensive entity and topical authority maps across 4 core SaaS product lines, targeting competitor content gaps and capturing middle-of-funnel (MOFU) and bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) purchase intent.

Phase 3: High-Intent Commercial Pivot

Rebuilt feature pages, comparison hubs, and solution pages. Rather than answering generic 'what is' questions, content addressed specific buyer pain points, pricing comparisons, and enterprise use cases.

Phase 4: GEO & AI Answer Visibility

Structured semantic entity nodes, schema markups, and external citation footprints so that AI engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) actively cite Vantage Circle as the category authority.

The Business Impact

While vanity informational pageviews recalibrated, lead volume and revenue never declined. In fact, organic lead contribution scaled from 16% (FY23) to 40% (FY25), cementing organic search as the primary B2B MQL engine, while Domain Rating reached 74.

Content Pruning Matrix

A systematic framework for evaluating legacy content against business value, crawl equity, and algorithmic health.

Topical Authority Mapping

Structuring the entire website around semantic entity graphs rather than isolated keyword targets.

  • Exhaustive competitive content gap audits across product lines.
  • Hub-and-spoke internal linking with optimized anchor text hierarchy.
  • Clear separation between educational, comparison, and product entities.
