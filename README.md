## 📂 Featured Case Study: The Post-HCU Strategic Pivot
> **"Informational traffic was vanity; qualified pipeline was sanity."**

When Google Helpful Content Updates and AI Overviews began summarizing broad informational queries, our playbook evolved:
1. **Aggressive Content Pruning:** De-indexed and retired hundreds of off-niche legacy blogs, restoring crawl equity to core revenue drivers.
2. **Topical Authority Mapping:** Built entity-driven topic clusters across 4 SaaS product lines (Rewards & Recognition, Pulse, Perks, Fit).
3. **High-Intent Commercial Pivot:** Shifted editorial focus to comparison hubs, product feature pages, and buyer-intent queries.
4. **GEO Citation Infrastructure:** Structured technical schemas and brand entity nodes for generative AI answer engines.

👉 **Outcome:** Inbound lead volume and revenue grew continuously year-over-year, scaling organic lead contribution to 40%.
